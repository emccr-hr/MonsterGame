*Full review on 2026-11-21 after Round 57. Earlier answered items are in Decisions-Log.md.*

## A. Big decisions ✅ ANSWERED (Round 61)
1. ✅ **No starter monster.** Start with 100 cages and catch your first one.
2. ✅ Only **Ansonville + one biome you pick** are open at the start.
3. ✅ Starting town is **Ansonville**, in the **center of the map**.
4. ✅ Buying a map part **opens the hidden biome** in that area.
5. ✅ Avatar's body is **made of the elements**.

## B. Yes/no checks on Claude's suggestions ✅ ALL ANSWERED YES (Round 58)
6. ✅ Scout keeps Peggy's **Cute** ability.
7. ✅ Slimy keeps Blobbin's **moves and ability**.
8. ✅ **Teleport pins:** a deleted pin comes back to your bag, and you can buy pins before level 50 (they work at 50+).
9. ✅ **Losing a battle** sends you to the last Reviver you used.
10. ✅ **Swapping team:** anytime outside battle; switching during battle uses your turn.
11. ✅ **Burn** = small damage for 2 turns. **Freeze** = 25% chance to skip a turn. These are the only 2 lasting effects.
12. ✅ **Fetch** = +20% attack, can stack twice, resets after battle.
13. ✅ **Dig** = hide underground 1 turn, then attack.
14. ✅ **Popsicle** heals the Snowman.
15. ✅ **Avatar can't be caught.**
16. ✅ **Blobbin's slime trail** is just for looks.
17. ✅ **Catch chance:** about 30% at full health, up to 90% at very low health. Knocked-out monsters can't be caught.
18. ✅ **Shop prices:** Cage 10, Potion 15, Wake-Up Snack 30, Map part 25–50, Teleport pin 100 *(designer)*.
