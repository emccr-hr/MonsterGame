# Blobbin

*Second monster designed (Round 9). Original creature from the designer. Named Blobbin by the designer (Round 10).*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | **Blobbin** |
| Home biome | **Volcano** |
| Spawn chance | **20%** of monster spawns in the Volcano (designer, Round 13) |
| Size | **Tall**, "kind of tall." Proposed: taller than it is wide, about medium-large height. |
| How it moves | Slides/oozes along (proposed). **Leaves a short trail of red slime** behind it. |
| Look | A **red blob** with a **goopy mouth**. |
| Ability (1) | **Lava Skin:** 50% chance to take **no damage** from a fire move. |
| Evolves into | **[Slimy](Slimy.md)** |
| Evolves at level X | **25** |
| Evolves from | Nothing, it's a first form |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goop Throw** | Throws a glob of goop at the other monster. | Medium |
| 2 | **Hit** | A basic body slam. Always hits. | Low |
| 3 | **Bite** | Chomps with its goopy mouth. | Medium |
| 4 | **Burn** | Burns the other monster: some damage now, plus a little damage each turn for 2 turns. | Medium (over time) |

*Fire moves (tagged 🔥): Burn.*

*Move names from the designer (Round 11). What each move does and its power are proposals.*

## Slime trail (designer: "a little bit amount of distance")
- Proposed: the trail is about 2–3 body-lengths long and fades away after a second or two.
- **Visual only**, so it doesn't hurt or slow anything. (✅ agreed, Round 58) See Flags X5 if he wants it to do something.

## Big Monster version
1.5x size: a very tall Blobbin with a longer slime trail. Small stat boost, star in the collection.

## Animation notes (for the developer)
- Idle: wobbles and jiggles; mouth drips goop.
- Moving: oozes forward, leaving slime blobs that shrink and fade.
