# Dumble

*First monster designed (Round 8). Original creature from the designer.*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Home biome | **Beach** |
| Spawn chance | **1 in 5 (20%)** of monster spawns at the Beach (designer, Round 19) |
| Size | **Small**, "fairly small, but not too small." Proposed: about 0.75x a medium monster; clearly bigger than the tiniest monsters. |
| How it moves | **Bounces** around instead of walking (hop, squish on landing, hop). |
| Look | A **dumpling** with **chopsticks for arms**. |
| Ability (1) | **"Not at this restaurant"**: not affected by an opponent's Eat. Eat used on Dumble does nothing. |
| Evolves into | **[Rom](Rom.md)** |
| Evolves at level X | **35** |
| Evolves from | Nothing, it's a first form |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bounce Attack** | Bounces up and lands on the other monster. | Medium |
| 2 | **Pinch** | Pinches with its chopstick arms. | Low–Medium |
| 3 | **Hit** | Basic body slam. Always hits. *(Same move Blobbin has.)* | Low |
| 4 | **Eat** | Takes a bite and heals Dumble a little (half the damage dealt). | Medium |

*Move names from the designer (Round 17). What each move does and its power are proposals.*

## Big Monster version
A Big Dumble is 1.5x size (that makes it roughly medium-large) with a small stat boost and a star in the collection.

## Animation notes (for the developer)
- Idle: small squishy bounce in place.
- Moving: hops from spot to spot instead of walking.
- Chopstick arms can wave or click together for attacks.
