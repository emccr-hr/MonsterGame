| # | Move | What it does | Power |

| 1 | **Protect the Queen** | Heals all your other team monsters. Proposed: +20% healing too (30% of max HP each). | None (team healing) |
| 2 | **Bite** | Chomps with pincers. **+20% damage** vs. Anti-Ant. | Medium+ |
