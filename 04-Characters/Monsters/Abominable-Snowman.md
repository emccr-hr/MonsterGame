# Abominable Snowman

*Yeti's evolution (Round 39). Original creature from the designer. Final form (2-form family by default).*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Home biome | Snowy (same as Yeti) |
| Spawn chance | **None.** Only obtained by evolving (designer, Round 52). |
| Size | **1.5x Yeti** ("the Yeti, and then half the Yeti stacked on top"). About 4.5x Dumble, the **biggest monster so far**. |
| How it moves | **Bounces** around (like Dumble, but huge). |
| Look | A **big snowman** with a **top hat**, **coal eyes**, **buttons**, **sticks for hands**, and a **mouth made of lots of pieces of coal**. |
| Ability (1) | **Fur Coat** (same as Yeti): cannot be frozen (designer, Round 42). |
| Evolves into | Nothing, **final form** |
| Evolves at level X | n/a |
| Evolves from | [Yeti](Yeti.md) at **level 40** |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Snowball** 🧊 | Throws a big snowball. | Medium |
| 2 | **Freeze** 🧊 | Icy blast. 25% chance the other monster is Frozen and skips its next turn. *(Same as Yeti's.)* | Medium |
| 3 | **Ice Slap** 🧊 | Slaps with an icy stick hand. | Medium–High |
| 4 | **Popsicle** | Munches a popsicle and **heals** 25% of max HP. (✅ agreed, Round 58) | None (healing) |

*Move names come from the designer (Round 42). Effects and power are proposals. 🧊 = Ice tag.*

## Evolution note
When Yeti evolves, its moves change: Bite → Snowball, Scratch → Ice Slap, Belly Flop → Popsicle. Freeze stays.

## Big Monster version
1.5x size: an enormous snowman. Small stat boost, star in the collection.

## Art note
The Yeti and the Abominable Snowman look very different (furry beast → snowman). That's fine and fun, but they need two separate drawings.
