# Yeti

*Fifth monster designed (Round 38). Original creature from the designer.*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Home biome | **Snowy** |
| Spawn chance | **30%** of monster spawns in the Snowy biome (designer, Round 41) |
| Size | **On the bigger side**, "decently big". Proposed: about 3x Dumble (bigger than Rom/Peggy, smaller than Scout). |
| How it moves | Proposed: stomps/lumbers on two legs |
| Look | A **white yeti** with **blue horns** and a **black face**. **Hair everywhere except the face.** |
| Ability (1) | **Fur Coat**: cannot be frozen. |
| Evolves into | **[Abominable Snowman](Abominable-Snowman.md)** |
| Evolves at level X | **40** |
| Evolves from | Nothing, it's a first form |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bite** | Chomps the other monster. *(Shared with Blobbin, Peggy, Anti-Ant.)* | Medium |
| 2 | **Scratch** | Claws at the other monster. | Low–Medium |
| 3 | **Belly Flop** | Jumps and lands belly-first on the other monster. | High |
| 4 | **Freeze** 🧊 | Blasts icy air. Some damage, plus a **25% chance** the other monster is **Frozen** and skips its next turn. | Medium |

*Move names come from the designer (Round 38). What each move does and its power are proposals (Flags X13).*

## Big Monster version
1.5x size (a giant yeti!), small stat boost, star in the collection.
