# Peggy

*Third monster designed (Round 23). Original creature from the designer.*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Home biome | **Forest** |
| Spawn chance | **5%** of monster spawns in the Forest (rare!) (designer, Round 29) |
| Size | **Medium**: **2x Dumble**, the same size as Rom (designer, Round 27). |
| How it moves | Proposed: waddles/trots like a bulldog |
| Look | An **English bulldog**, **brown** with **white spots on her neck and head**. |
| Ability (1) | **Cute**: each turn, **5 in 100 (5%)** chance the opponent doesn't attack. |
| Evolves into | **[Scout](Scout.md)** |
| Evolves at level X | **45** |
| Evolves from | Nothing, it's a first form |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bite** | Chomps the other monster. *(Same move Blobbin has.)* | Medium |
| 2 | **Lick** | A big sloppy lick. | Low |
| 3 | **Slobber** | Drools all over the other monster. Proposed: small damage, and the other monster's next attack is a little weaker. | Low |
| 4 | **Sleep** | Peggy takes a nap and **heals**, but **cannot attack next turn** (confirmed by designer, Round 24). Proposed heal: half of max HP. | None (healing) |

*Move names from the designer (Round 23). What each move does and its power are proposals. See Flags X8.*

## Big Monster version
1.5x size, small stat boost, star in the collection. A Big Peggy is a very large bulldog.
