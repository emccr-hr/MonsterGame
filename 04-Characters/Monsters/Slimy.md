# Slimy

*Blobbin's evolution (Round 15). Original creature from the designer.*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Home biome | Volcano (same as Blobbin) |
| Spawn chance | **None.** Only obtained by evolving (designer, Round 52). |
| Size | **Slightly bigger than Blobbin**, so still tall. Proposed: about 1.2x Blobbin. |
| How it moves | Oozes, and leaves a red slime trail like Blobbin (proposed: slightly longer). |
| Look | A bigger Blobbin in a **slightly darker red**, with the same goopy mouth. |
| Ability (1) | **Lava Skin** (same as Blobbin): 50% chance to ignore fire moves. (✅ agreed, Round 58) |
| Evolves into | Nothing, **final form** |
| Evolves at level X | n/a |
| Evolves from | [Blobbin](Blobbin.md) at **level 25** |

## Moves (max 4)
Keeps Blobbin's moves (Goop Throw, Hit, Bite, Burn), and they hit a bit harder because Slimy's stats are higher. (✅ agreed, Round 58)

| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goop Throw | Throws a glob of goop. | Medium |
| 2 | Hit | Basic body slam. Always hits. | Low |
| 3 | Bite | Chomps with its goopy mouth. | Medium |
| 4 | Burn 🔥 | Damage now, plus a little each turn for 2 turns. | Medium (over time) |

## Big Monster version
1.5x size, small stat boost, star in the collection.

## Art note
Same drawing as Blobbin, just bigger and a darker red. The developer can reuse Blobbin's art with a color change until the designer draws Slimy.
