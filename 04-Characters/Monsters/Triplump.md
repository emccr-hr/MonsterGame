# Triplump

*Camelump's evolution. Claude-made (Round 54) at the designer's request. Final form (2-form family).*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Animal | camel (evolved) |
| Home biome | Desert (same as Camelump) |
| Spawn chance | **None.** Only obtained by evolving (Round 52 rule). |
| Size | Huge (4x Dumble) |
| How it moves | Slow, heavy sway; humps jiggle. |
| Look | A giant camel with **three wobbly humps** and an even longer set of eyelashes. |
| Ability (1) | **Water Storage**: heals 5% of max HP at the end of every turn. *(same as Camelump)* |
| Evolves into | Nothing, **final form** |
| Evolves at level X | n/a |
| Evolves from | [Camelump](Camelump.md) at **level 30** |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Desert Charge 🪨** | Charges across the sand and rams. *(New. Replaces Camelump's Spit.)* | High |
| 2 | **Stomp 🪨** | Stomps with a heavy hoof. Hits harder than before. | High |
| 3 | **Hit** | Basic body slam. Always hits. Hits harder than before. | Low |
| 4 | **Sleep** | Naps to heal, but cannot attack next turn. Hits harder than before. | None (healing) |

## Evolution note
When Camelump evolves, **Spit is replaced by Desert Charge** automatically.
