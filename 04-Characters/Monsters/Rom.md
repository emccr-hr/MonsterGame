# Rom

*Dumble's evolution (Round 20). Original creature from the designer.*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Home biome | Beach (same as Dumble) |
| Spawn chance | **None.** Only obtained by evolving (designer, Round 52). |
| Size | **Twice as big as Dumble** (about 1.5x a medium monster). |
| How it moves | **Walks** on two legs made of **woven-together noodles**. |
| Look | A **ramen bowl** with **chopsticks for arms**, **woven noodle legs**, and **egg shoes** (like the soft-boiled egg in ramen). |
| Ability (1) | **"Not at this restaurant"**: not affected by an opponent's Eat (same as Dumble). |
| Evolves into | Nothing, **final form** |
| Evolves at level X | n/a |
| Evolves from | [Dumble](Dumble.md) at **level 35** |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Splash** | Splashes hot ramen broth on the other monster. *(New. Replaces Dumble's Bounce Attack.)* | Medium–High |
| 2 | **Pinch** | Pinches with chopstick arms. | Medium |
| 3 | **Hit** | Basic body slam. Always hits. | Low |
| 4 | **Eat** | Takes a bite and heals a little (half the damage dealt). | Medium |

*Move names from the designer (Round 20). What each move does and its power are proposals. Rom's stats are higher than Dumble's, so shared moves hit harder.*

## Animation notes (for the developer)
- Walking: wobbly noodle legs step forward; the egg shoes go tap-tap.
- Idle: steam rises from the bowl; chopsticks twitch.

## Evolution note
When Dumble evolves, **Bounce Attack is replaced by Splash** automatically. No "forget a move" choice is needed.

## Big Monster version
1.5x size (huge!), small stat boost, star in the collection.
