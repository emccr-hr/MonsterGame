## Notes
- Deep Freeze is a new kind of ability (`freezeChanceBonus` in `abilities.json`): it adds 15% to the freeze chance of moves that can freeze. Bite and Kick never freeze.
- With Deep Freeze, Freeze Punch freezes 25% of the time and Freeze Breath 40%.
- Stats (size rule, 4x): HP 62, Attack 50, Defense 50, Speed 38.
