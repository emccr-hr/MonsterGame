# Anti-Ant

*Fourth monster designed (Round 30). Original creature from the designer. Named Anti-Ant by the designer (Round 33).*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | **Anti-Ant** |
| Home biome | **Desert** (designer, Round 31) |
| Spawn chance | **40%** of monster spawns in the Desert (common) (designer, Round 32) |
| Size | **Same as Rom (2x Dumble), medium** (designer, Round 37). "Big" for an ant. *(Not the same as a "Big Monster", the rare 1-in-100 version.)* |
| How it moves | Proposed: scuttles on six legs |
| Look | A **big red ant**. |
| Ability (1) | **Colony Bond**: not attacked by other red ants. See Flags X11. |
| Evolves into | **[Queen Ant](Queen-Ant.md)** |
| Evolves at level X | **15** |
| Evolves from | Nothing, it's a first form |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Protect the Queen** | **Heals all your other monsters** on your team (the ones not fighting right now). Proposed: each gets 25% of max HP back. | None (team healing) |
| 2 | **Bite** | Chomps with its pincers. *(Same move Blobbin and Peggy have.)* | Medium |
| 3 | **Sting** | Stings the other monster. | Medium |
| 4 | **Dig** | (✅ agreed, Round 58) Digs underground this turn (can't be hit), then pops up and attacks next turn. | High (takes 2 turns) |

*Move names and Protect the Queen's effect come from the designer (Round 30). Exact numbers and Dig/Sting details are proposals (Flags X12).*
