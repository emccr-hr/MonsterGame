# Smokewing

*Ashflap's evolution. Claude-made (Round 54) at the designer's request. Final form (2-form family).*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Animal | bat (evolved) |
| Home biome | Volcano (same as Ashflap) |
| Spawn chance | **None.** Only obtained by evolving (Round 52 rule). |
| Size | Medium (2x Dumble) |
| How it moves | Swoops in wide loops; smoke clouds behind. |
| Look | A big bat whose **wings are made of swirling smoke**, with glowing red eyes. |
| Ability (1) | **Wise**: takes 10% less damage from every move. *(same as Ashflap)* |
| Evolves into | Nothing, **final form** |
| Evolves at level X | n/a |
| Evolves from | [Ashflap](Ashflap.md) at **level 30** |

## Moves (max 4)
| # | Move | What it does | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bite** | Little fang bite. Hits harder than before. | Medium |
| 2 | **Ash Cloud 🔥** | Flaps a cloud of hot ash. Hits harder than before. | Medium |
| 3 | **Screech** | Loud screech. The other monster's next attack is a bit weaker. Hits harder than before. | None |
| 4 | **Cinder Dive 🔥** | Dives down wrapped in hot cinders. *(New. Replaces Ashflap's Hit.)* | Medium–High |

## Evolution note
When Ashflap evolves, **Hit is replaced by Cinder Dive** automatically.
