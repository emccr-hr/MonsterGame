## Notes
- **How "1/4 more damage" works:** damage goes up with the Attack stat, so Megalodon's Attack is 63, a quarter more than Mr. Toothache's 50. His moves stay the same shared moves; no new rule.
- Stats (evolved total 280, size rule for 5x): HP 102, Attack 63, Defense 70, Speed 45.
