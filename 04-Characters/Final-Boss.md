# Final Boss: Avatar

*Designed in Round 43. Beat it to see the ending (Decisions-Log: win goal). After the ending the game keeps going: back in Ansonville, keep catching (Round 63).*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | **Avatar** (designer, Round 47) |
| Concept | A **combination of all 5 elements**, one for each biome (designer, Rounds 43 and 47). |
| Where | **The center of the map, where all 5 biomes connect** (Round 48): a gated arena in **Ansonville** (Round 61, Flags C7). |
| How to reach | The center gate opens after you **catch every monster from every biome** (designer, Round 50). Only monsters that **spawn naturally** count; evolved forms don't (Round 52). |
| Look | A **human shape with a body made of the 5 elements** (Rounds 49 and 61): e.g., one arm of lava 🔥, one arm of ice 🧊, legs of rock and sand 🪨, vines and leaves for hair 🌿, water swirling around it 💧. **Must be an original design** (see note below). |
| Size | **10x Dumble** (designer, Round 49), the biggest thing in the game. More than twice the Abominable Snowman (about 4.5x). |
| Ability | **None** (designer, Round 56). Its 5 elemental moves are its strength. |
| Catchable? | **No**, it's a boss. (✅ agreed, Round 58) |

## Moves: one from each element (5 moves, a boss exception)
The designer asked Claude to pick **one attack from each monster family, one for each element**. The boss has **5 moves**. Normal monsters are capped at 4, but the boss is special. Picks:

| Element | Move | Taken from | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Fire | **Burn** | Blobbin / Slimy (Volcano) | Damage now, plus a little each turn for 2 turns. |
| 💧 Water | **Splash** | Rom (Beach) | Splashes the other monster. Medium–High. |
| 🪨 Earth | **Dig** | Anti-Ant / Queen Ant (Desert) | Goes underground for a turn, then attacks (see X12). |
| 🧊 Ice | **Freeze** | Yeti / Abominable Snowman (Snowy) | Icy blast; 25% chance to freeze for a turn. |
| 🌿 Nature | **Fetch** | Scout (Forest) | Powers up: +20% attack, up to 2 times. The boss gets stronger the longer the fight goes! |

*Claude picked Fetch for Nature because it's the Forest family's signature move. The designer can swap it for Bite or Lick.*

## Design note (for the developer)
"Avatar" is the designer's chosen name and an everyday word. The boss must be an **original elemental monster** made from this game's 5 biomes. Do **not** use the look, characters, symbols, or story of any TV show or movie with a similar name (Flags N2).

Boss versions of these moves hit harder (proposed: +30%).
