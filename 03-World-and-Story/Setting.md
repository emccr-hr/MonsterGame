# Setting

## Where & when
A world split into **5 biomes** (Forest, Beach, Desert, Snowy, Volcano) around the starting town, **Ansonville**, in the center.

## Map layout (designer, Rounds 48 and 61)
**Ansonville**, the starting town, is in the **center of the map**, where all 5 biomes connect. Avatar's arena is also at the center: a **gated arena inside Ansonville** (see Flags C7).

```
             Snowy
      Desert   |   Volcano
           \   |   /
         [ ANSONVILLE ]
         (Avatar arena,
          gated)
           /       \
      Forest ----- Beach
```
*(Sketch only. Exact positions are up to the developer.)*

- **Start:** only Ansonville and **one biome the player picks** are open.
- **Other biomes:** hidden until you **buy their map part**.
- **Avatar arena gate:** opens after catching one of every naturally spawning monster (Round 52).

## Biomes
| # | Biome | Look / feel | Monsters found here | Unlock order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forest | Trees, grass, flowers | Peggy, Bushtail, Hootle | Pick one to start, or open with its map part |
| 2 | Beach | Sand, waves, palm trees | Dumble, Sidesnap, Squawkle | Pick one to start, or open with its map part |
| 3 | Desert | Dunes, cactus, ruins | Anti-Ant, Stingtail, Camelump | Pick one to start, or open with its map part |
| 4 | Snowy | Snow, ice, pine trees | Yeti, Pengwiggle, Frostpaw | Pick one to start, or open with its map part |
| 5 | Volcano | Lava, rocks, smoke | Blobbin, Cinderscale, Ashflap | Pick one to start, or open with its map part |

Names come from the designer (Round 6). There is **no fixed order**: the player picks the first biome and opens the rest with map parts (Round 61).

**Rule (designer):** every monster belongs to exactly one biome. You have to visit all 5 to catch them all.

## Tone
Fun, friendly adventure.

## Rules of the world
- Every biome and Ansonville has a **shop with a Reviver** next to it (free full heal).
- **Biome towns (Round 90):** each biome has a little town at its top, with houses built from that biome's own wood, and its shop and Reviver are there: **Forest Village** (Forest), **Water City** (Beach), **Dune City** (Desert), **Ice Town** (Snowy) and **Lava Town** (Volcano).
- Wild monsters appear only in their home biome.
- Big Monster versions can appear in that same biome (1 in 100).
