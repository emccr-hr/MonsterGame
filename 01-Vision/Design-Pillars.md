# Design Pillars

1. **Catching is fun.** Throwing a cage and watching it grow should feel great every time.
2. **My monsters, my world.** Everything comes from Magic and Monsters, the designer's own creation.
3. **Simple to play.** A 10-year-old can pick it up with no instructions.
4. **Always something to work toward.** The next level, the next task, the next evolution, the next Big Monster.
