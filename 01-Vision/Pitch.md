# Pitch

## One-sentence hook
Explore a world full of Magic Monsters, catch them in tiny cages that grow when thrown, train them through battles, and finish their special tasks so they can evolve.

## Title
**Magic and Monsters**, based on the designer's own card game of the same name.

## Genre
Monster-catching adventure with turn-based battles. It's the same genre as the game he named as inspiration, but every monster, item, name and piece of art is original (see Flags.md, N1).

## Platform(s)
Web browser on a computer, keyboard controls. 2D top-down.

## Target player
The designer (age 10), his family and friends.

## What makes it different
- The monsters come from his own card game, **Magic and Monsters**.
- **Cages** instead of capture balls: a tiny cage that grows into a big cage when thrown.
- **Big Monsters** are the rare special version: a giant-size monster you can find occasionally.
- **Evolution by tasks**: each monster has its own list of things to do before it can evolve.

## Similar in feel to
Simple, friendly monster-catching games: walk around, find monsters, catch them, battle, level up. We match the *feel* only. No names, characters, maps or art are copied.
