Magic and Monsters: art mockups

A visual read of what the design docs say the game should look like, as of 2026-11-21 (worktree MonsterGame-m3).

Every picture on this page is a proposal or placeholder, not final art. The plan is that the designer's own drawings replace them. Anything the docs don't say is marked mockup guess.

2. Monsters by biome

All 15 base forms (the ones that spawn in the wild). In each row, monsters are drawn to scale with each other, with Dumble = 1x. Colors and shapes come from each monster's "Look" line.

Owner decided, 2026-11-21 "2x Dumble" means twice as tall and twice as wide (uniform scale). Each drawing sits in a square box N times the size of Dumble's box. Where a monster's colors aren't given (Dumble, Hootle's leaf tufts), the color is a guess.

Size lineup: smallest to biggest

Uniform scale: each dotted square is N Dumble-boxes wide and N tall. Evolved forms are placeholders (a colored shape with a name and an icon, as the Development Plan says). Scout is "4x but skinny", so its box is 4x4 with a lean shape inside. Avatar is drawn because it has its own mockup below.

3. Peggy: vector vs. pixel art

Same Peggy (Peggy.md: a brown English bulldog with white spots on her neck and head), same pose, same colors, same size on screen. Left: vector, smooth SVG shapes. Right: pixel, a real 32x32 grid of square cells, drawn crisp and scaled up.

For a fair comparison, the pixel grid was sampled automatically from the vector drawing, using Peggy's 5 colors. A hand-placed pixel version would be cleaner. 96 px is close to Peggy's battle size from the rule in section 7 (102 px), rounded to a whole 3x zoom.

4. Characters

Avatar (final boss)

  • Human shape, body made of the 5 elements (Final-Boss.md, Rounds 49 and 61).
  • Lava arm, ice arm, legs of rock and sand, vine-and-leaf hair, water swirling around (Final-Boss.md "Look", given as examples).
  • Size 10x Dumble (Round 49).
  • mockup guess Stone torso and head, glowing eyes, which arm is on which side. The docs don't describe the face or chest.
  • Kept away from any show: no robes, no head markings, no symbols, no animal companion (Flags N2).

Player character

Owner decided, 2026-11-21 Working look: a boy with shoulder-length blonde hair, light skin and blue eyes. mockup guess Outfit: blue jacket, yellow scarf, red backpack, gray pants, brown boots. Characters.md still proposes picking from 3 to 4 looks and typing a name (max 10 letters). The designer still gets a say (Open-Questions 37).

Dumble's map size (about 1 tile) is a mockup guess.

The guide

Owner decided, 2026-11-21 The guide is a bald man with a dark mustache. He explains the game when needed and points the player toward quests and goals. He stands next to the Reviver in Ansonville (Characters.md; ADR 0004). mockup guess Outfit: green vest, cream shirt, brown pants, a rolled-up map in his hand. His name is still open, so for now he's just "the guide".

Battle size rule

Owner decided, 2026-11-21 In battle, every monster shrinks so a 10x Avatar fits, and Dumble gets smaller too. proposed rule Use a square root, so bigger monsters still look bigger, just not 10 times bigger:

battle size (px) = clamp(72 × √N, 56, 240)  on the 960×540 screen. N is the size vs. Dumble (multiply by 1.5 first for a Big Monster).

MonsterN√NBattle size
Dumble11.0072 px
Big Dumble1.51.2288 px
Peggy, Squawkle21.41102 px
Yeti, Camelump31.73125 px
Scout42.00144 px
Abominable Snowman4.52.12153 px
Avatar103.16228 px

The Avatar-to-Dumble ratio drops from 10 to 1 down to about 3 to 1, so both fit on screen. The 56 px floor keeps any future tiny monster readable. The 240 px cap leaves room for the info cards. Map sprites keep the true sizes.
