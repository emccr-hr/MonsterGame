# Core Loop

## Moment-to-moment (seconds)
Walk around and spot a wild Magic Monster. Then choose: **throw a cage now** (riskier) or **battle it down** to low HP and then throw (safer, and earns XP). Watch the tiny cage grow big and see if it holds.

## Session loop (minutes)
Explore an area → catch new monsters → battle wild monsters / trainers → monsters gain levels → tick off evolution tasks → evolve.

## Long-term loop (hours)
Fill your collection of every monster, find Big versions, evolve your whole team, catch every wild monster to open the center gate, then beat **Avatar**.
