# Progression & Economy

## How the player grows
Two separate systems that work together:
- **Levels** (from defeating monsters) make a monster stronger and teach it new moves.
- **Tasks** (a per-monster checklist) unlock **evolution**.
- Proposed rule: levels feed tasks. For example, "Reach level 10" can be one of the tasks. That way both systems point the same direction (see F2).

## Evolution task types
**v1 decision: only #1 (Reach level N).** The others are saved for later versions.

Keep this to a small set of things the game can easily count:
1. Reach level N
2. Win N battles
3. Defeat N monsters of a certain kind
4. Be caught / used as a Big Monster *(optional)*
5. Use a specific move N times

## Resources / currencies
- **Magic Gems:** earned by winning battles (wild 5–10, trainer 30).
- **Starting items:** **100 cages** (designer, Round 61) and **100 Magic Gems** (Round 5). See Flags C8.
- **Cages:** about 10 Magic Gems each, so the starting money buys up to 10.
- **Shops:** one in **Ansonville** (the starting town) **and one in each biome**. Same shop screen everywhere. Every shop sells map parts for biomes you haven't opened yet.
- **Shop prices** (✅ agreed, Round 58):

| Item | Price | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Cage | 10 | Catch a monster |
| Healing potion | 15 | Restore 20 HP to one monster |
| Wake-Up Snack | 30 | Bring back a knocked-out monster at half HP |
| Map part | 50 (proposed) | **Opens the hidden biome** in that part of the map (designer, Round 61). One per locked biome. Sold in every shop, including Ansonville. |
| Teleport pin | **100** (designer) | Place on the map and teleport there (level 50+). Max 5 placed. |

- **Currency name:** **Magic Gems** (designer, Round 7).

## Unlocks
- **Map parts:** buying a map part **opens the biome hidden in that area** (designer, Round 61). Collect all 4 to open the whole map.
- **Teleporting (level 50+):** teleport to every shop, and place up to 5 teleport pins bought at shops (designer, Round 44). See Mechanics.md.

## Win / lose conditions
- Battle lost: go back to the **last Reviver** you used, with your monsters healed. No permanent loss.
- **To reach the boss:** catch one of every **naturally spawning** monster (base forms). Evolutions don't count (Rounds 50, 52).
- **Ending:** beat Avatar, the final boss.
- **Bonus goal:** catch every form, including evolutions and Big Monsters (proposed, C6).
